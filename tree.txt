Structure:
                                      ID(1)
                                         /\
                               ID(2)       ID(3)
                                 /\              /\
                          ID(4) ID(5) ID(6) ID(7)

Example:
                                         &&
                                         /  \
                                     ==    matches
                                     / \     /    \
                                  _id  23  title  why*

&&
 |_ ==

       | ==
 |     |_ 23
 |_ matches
    |_ title
    |_ why*
